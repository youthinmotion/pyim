Project Youth in Motion

PYIM is a fiscally sponsored 501(c)(3) under The Hack Foundation that works to empower underserved communities through health education and sports equipment. The idea was thought of by a high school freshman, who hopes to make a difference in children's lives.

About Us

Our Mission At Project Youth In Motion, we believe every child deserves the opportunity to live a healthy, active life. Through health literacy, healthcare education, and sports equity initiatives, we work to empower underserved youth - especially those in immigrant and low-income communities - with the knowledge, resources, and confidence to thrive. Together, we’re breaking barriers to healthcare and sports participation, one child at a time.
